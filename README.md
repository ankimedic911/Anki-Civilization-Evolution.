🌍 Civilization Evolution – A Gamified Anki Add-on! 🚀
Turn your studying into an epic civilization-building game! 🎮✨

📜 Overview
Civilization Evolution is an Anki add-on that makes flashcard reviewing more engaging and rewarding. Every time you study, you earn XP, and your civilization progresses from primitive hunter-gatherers to an interstellar empire!

🔥 Features
✅ XP-Based Progression: Earn XP when you review flashcards (Good = +0.2 XP, Easy = +0.4 XP).
✅ Pixel-Art Evolution: Unlock beautifully designed civilization stages.
✅ Pop-Up Milestone Alerts: Get a notification when you level up!
✅ Persistent XP Tracking: Your XP and level are saved even when Anki is restarted.
✅ Global XP Bar: View your progress across all decks, no reset per deck.
✅ Quest System (Experimental!): A new system introduces guidance and challenges to enhance the experience.

🛠 Installation Instructions
1️⃣ Download the Add-on
📂 [Download civilizationevolution.zip](Insert Link Here)

2️⃣ Extract and Install
Extract the civilizationevolution folder.
Move it to:
Windows: %APPDATA%\Anki2\addons21\
Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/Anki2/addons21/
Linux: ~/.local/share/Anki2/addons21/
Restart Anki.
3️⃣ Merge the Image Folders
Due to file size limits, image files are split into two folders (resources and resources2).

🔹 To make the add-on work properly, move all images from resources2 into the resources folder.
🔹 After merging the images, you can delete the empty resources2 folder.

✅ Now all level-up images should display correctly in Anki!

📢 How It Works
🔹 XP System: Your civilization evolves based on the XP you earn in Anki.
🔹 Milestone Pop-ups: At certain XP thresholds, you receive a visual pop-up displaying your civilization’s progress.
🔹 Global XP Tracking: Your XP is cumulative across all decks.
🔹 Quest System (Work in Progress!): The system offers civilization-building guidance and future challenges.

⚠️ Note: Some images contain AI-generated text that may not be perfectly clear.
🔹 To improve readability, important information will be summarized in the pop-ups!

📢 Important User Tip: Click Slower Near Level-Up
📢 If you're near a level-up threshold (for example, 19.80 XP, and the next review will trigger a level-up),
✅ Click your answer button slowly!
This prevents the pop-up from disappearing instantly before you see your civilization evolve.
