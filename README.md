# Anki-Civilization-Evolution.
🌍 Civilization Evolution – A Gamified Anki Add-on! 🚀 
Turn your studying into an epic civilization-building game! 🎮
📜 Overview
Civilization Evolution is an Anki add-on that makes flashcard reviewing more engaging and rewarding. Every time you study, you earn XP and watch your civilization progress from primitive hunter-gatherers to an interstellar empire!

🔥 Features
✅ XP-Based Progression: Earn XP when you review flashcards (Good = +0.2 XP, Easy = +0.4 XP).
✅ Pixel-Art Evolution: Unlock beautifully designed civilization stages.
✅ Pop-Up Milestone Alerts: Get a notification when you level up!
✅ Persistent XP Tracking: Your XP and level are saved even when Anki is restarted.
✅ Global XP Bar: View your progress across all decks, no reset per deck.
✅ Quest Info Unlocks: Gain insights into each historical stage as you progress.

📢 How It Works
🔹 XP System: Your civilization evolves based on the XP you earn in Anki.
🔹 Milestone Pop-ups: At certain XP thresholds, you receive a visual pop-up displaying your civilization’s progress.
🔹 Global XP Tracking: Your XP is cumulative across all decks.

📜 Civilization Progression Stages
🌱 Level 1-10: The Dawn of Humanity (Pre-Homo Sapiens)
🔥 Level 11-20: Early Homo Sapiens (Advanced Tool Use & Survival)
🏹 Level 21-30: Advanced Hunting & Tribal Life
🌾 Level 31-40: Early Settlements & Complex Society
🚀 Level 41-50: Towards the Neolithic Revolution
